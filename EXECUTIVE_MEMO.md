# Executive Memo — Olist Marketplace Analysis

**To:** CEO, Olist Marketplace
**From:** Ayush Biswas, Data Analyst
**Date:** September 2026
**Re:** Performance review — revenue, delivery and customers (Sep 2016 to Aug 2018)

## Bottom line

Revenue stands at R$13.22M and deliveries keep their promise 92% of the time, but three risks sit underneath. First, 97% of customers never buy a second time, so the business runs entirely on new acquisition. Second, a late delivery cuts customer trust by 1.7 stars. Third, 49% of all revenue comes from just 16% of customers, which means a small group carries half the business.

## Findings

**1. Growth was strong and has now flattened.** Total delivered revenue is R$13.22M. Revenue grew roughly 8x during 2017 (January R$112K to a November peak of R$988K). The November peak is Black Friday, confirmed three separate ways: daily orders spiked 5x on the 24th, revenue peaked that month, and month-over-month growth hit +52%. Since early 2018 revenue has moved sideways between R$826K and R$978K a month.

**2. Revenue is concentrated in one region.** Sao Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais together produce 63.4% of revenue, and Sao Paulo alone produces 38.3%. The top 10 of 71 product categories carry 62% of revenue.

**3. Late delivery destroys trust.** 91.89% of orders arrive on time. The remaining 8.11% average 2.57 stars against 4.29 for on-time orders, a penalty of 1.7 stars per late experience. Regional spread is extreme: northern states wait up to 29.3 days against 8.7 days in Sao Paulo, 3.4 times slower.

**4. Customers do not come back.** Only about 3.4% of customers ever place a second order, and monthly cohort retention after the first month is roughly 0.5%. Of 93,358 analyzed customers, only 1,241 (1.3%) qualify as Champions.

**5. Half the money sits with a small group.** Big Spenders are 16% of customers but 49% of revenue (R$6.5M). The largest group by headcount, New and Fresh customers (34%), holds only 20% of revenue.

**6. Our volume category has a quality problem.** bed_bath_table is the number 3 revenue category (R$1.02M, most items sold at 10,953) and also sits on the worst-satisfaction list at 3.92 stars across 10,985 reviews. We are winning volume in a category where customers are unhappy.

## Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Based on | Expected impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Add a logistics partner (3PL) or regional warehouse for northern routes (RR, AP, AM) | Finding 3: those routes take 26 to 29 days, 3.4x slower than Sao Paulo | Cutting delivery to around 20 days should reduce bad reviews in those states and open room for order growth | High |
| 2 | Win-back campaign for the 17,373 Sleeping customers (silent for a year or more) with a discount or free shipping coupon | Finding 4: they already trusted us once, and reactivation is cheaper than acquisition | A 2 to 3 percent reactivation recovers 400 to 500 customers and lifts repeat rate from 3.4% toward 5% | Low |
| 3 | VIP program for the 14,504 Big Spenders: early access, priority support, personal offers | Finding 5: 16% of customers carry 49% of revenue | Protecting even 10% of this group protects roughly R$650K of revenue | Medium |
| 4 | Quality audit of bed_bath_table: packaging damage, seller standards and product descriptions | Finding 6: top-3 revenue with bottom-10 satisfaction | Moving 3.92 stars to 4.2 protects our number 3 revenue line from competitors | Medium |

## Caveats

- Data covers Brazil, prices in Brazilian reais, September 2016 to August 2018.
- Revenue counts delivered orders only (97.02% of all orders).
- Recent cohorts show low retention because their observation window is short, not because loyalty dropped.
- Customer analysis covers the 93,358 people with at least one delivered order.
